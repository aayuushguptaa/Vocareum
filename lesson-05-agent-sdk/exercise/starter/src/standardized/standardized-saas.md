# SaaS Contract - Standardized Format

## Contract Summary

### Parties
- **Vendor:** CloudTech Solutions Inc.
- **Customer:** Acme Corporation

### Term
- **Effective Date:** January 15, 2024
- **Duration:** 12 months

### Financial Terms
- **Payment Amount:** $2,500 per month (Annual: $30,000)
- **Payment Frequency:** Annual in advance
- **Payment Schedule:** Annual invoice due within 30 days of invoice date

### Legal Terms
- **Liability Cap:** Limited to fees paid in the prior 12 months; excludes indirect, consequential, and punitive damages
- **Termination Notice:** 60 days written notice (standard termination)
- **Termination Conditions:**
  - Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice
  - Customer may terminate immediately if Provider breaches and fails to cure within 30 days

### IP & Data
- **IP Ownership:**
  - Provider retains all IP rights to the platform
  - Customer retains rights to their data
- **Data Protection:**
  - Data stored in US data centers
  - Provider will delete data within 30 days of termination upon customer request
- **Confidentiality:**
  - Both parties maintain confidentiality of proprietary information for 3 years following termination

### Special Provisions
- Cloud-based project management platform service
- Cure period: 30 days for Provider to remedy breach before immediate termination

### Risk Assessment
- **Missing Terms:**
  - No explicit Service Level Agreement (SLA) or uptime guarantee specified
  - No backup and disaster recovery procedures defined
  - No security standards or certifications mentioned (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001)
  - No audit rights for Customer to verify compliance
  - No warranty disclaimers explicitly stated
  - No force majeure clause included
  - No renewal terms specified (auto-renew vs. manual renewal)
  - No price increase or adjustment terms for future periods

- **Ambiguous/Unclear Terms:**
  - "Cure within 30 days" applies only to Customer termination rights; unclear if this applies to Provider's breach remediation
  - No definition of what constitutes "proprietary information" for confidentiality purposes
  - No specification of data format for deletion (hard delete, secure wipe, etc.)
  - No performance metrics or service availability commitments

- **Red Flags:**
  - Relatively short liability cap (12-month fees) may be insufficient for critical business services
  - No indemnification clause for third-party IP infringement claims
  - Limited data security and compliance requirements specified
  - No transition assistance clause for data migration after termination
  - No definition of "user" limits or concurrent access restrictions
  - Confidentiality period (3 years) is relatively short for some industries
