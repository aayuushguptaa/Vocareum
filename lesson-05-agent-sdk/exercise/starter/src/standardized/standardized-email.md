  - No liability limitations or caps defined

  - No intellectual property ownership clause (who owns the redesigned website?)

- ⚠️ **High-Risk Issues:**
  - This is an informal email proposal, not a formal contract
  - No signatures or formal agreement mechanism specified
  - Ambiguous what "completion" means - no definition of done/acceptance
  - 50/50 split payment creates risk if project stalls
  - No protection for either party if the other party defaults
  - No protection for vendor if customer cancels mid-project
  - No protection for customer if deliverables are unsatisfactory

- 🔴 **Recommended Actions:**
  - Create a formal statement of work (SOW) detailing all deliverables and acceptance criteria
  - Define intellectual property ownership explicitly
  - Add comprehensive liability and indemnification clauses
  - Include termination conditions and notice periods
  - Specify dispute resolution mechanisms
  - Add confidentiality and data protection clauses
  - Include warranty period for delivered work
  - Define payment milestones beyond just "deposit" and "completion"
